Privacy Policy – Sprout Knight: Horde Survivor

Developer: Mossforge Games
Contact: mossforgegames@gmail.com
Last updated: 16 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game "Sprout Knight: Horde Survivor" ("the game") handles information.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask you for your name, email address or any account. There is no sign-in. Your game progress (such as gold, unlocked heroes, upgrades and settings) is saved only on your own device. We do not have access to it, and it is not sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID or the Apple IDFA (only with your permission on iPhone),
  • your IP address and approximate location (derived from the IP address),
  • information about how you interact with ads and the game (for example ad views and clicks, app launches),
  • diagnostic and performance information (for example crash logs).

This information is collected and processed by Google as our ad provider. You can learn how Google uses information here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Your choices and consent

  • If you are in the UK, European Economic Area or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later in the game's settings.
  • On iPhone, the game asks for permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads will not be personalised.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • You can choose to watch optional rewarded ads in exchange for in-game rewards. These are always optional.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Game progress is stored only on your device. You can delete it at any time by deleting the game's data or uninstalling the game. For information collected by Google AdMob, see Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). See also: Delete your data.

6. Security

Data sent by the advertising SDK is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date it was last updated.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: mossforgegames@gmail.com
