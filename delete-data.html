Delete your data – Sprout Knight: Horde Survivor

Developer: Mossforge Games · Contact: mossforgegames@gmail.com

The game has no accounts and never asks for your name or email.

Your game progress

Your progress is saved on your phone. To delete it, uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone's settings. If you use cloud save, also delete the game's saved data in your iCloud settings (iPhone) or Google Play Games settings (Android).

Anonymous gameplay data and crash reports

If you agreed to share anonymous gameplay data, it is linked only to a random ID made on your phone. To have it deleted:

1. In the game, open Settings → Send feedback and copy the "Player" line.

2. Email it to mossforgegames@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".

We will delete the data linked to that ID within 30 days. Otherwise, gameplay data is deleted automatically after up to 26 months and crash reports after up to 90 days.

You can stop sharing at any time in Settings → "Share anonymous gameplay data".

Advertising data

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and manage Google's ad data at https://myadcenter.google.com
