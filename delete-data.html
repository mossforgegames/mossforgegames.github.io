Delete your data – Sprout Knight: Horde Survivor (Mossforge Games)

Sprout Knight: Horde Survivor has no accounts. Your game progress is stored only on your own device.

How to delete your data

  1. Game data: uninstall the game, or go to your phone's Settings > Apps > Sprout Knight > Storage > Clear data. This permanently deletes all your progress (gold, heroes, upgrades and settings).
  2. Advertising data: on Android, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads and delete or reset your Advertising ID. Data processed by Google AdMob is handled under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  3. Questions or help: email mossforgegames@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and we will help you within 30 days.

What is deleted: all game progress and settings stored on your device.

What is kept: we do not keep any personal data on our own servers.
